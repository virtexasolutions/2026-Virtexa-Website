No matter how organized you are, you will miss calls. You are driving to a showing, sitting across the table at a closing, or finally having dinner with your family. The problem is not the missed call itself. It is what happens next: many callers do not leave a voicemail, and by the time you call back, they have already reached another agent.

**Missed-call text-back** is one of the simplest ways to fix that. This guide explains how it works, what a good message looks like, and how to use it responsibly.

## What is missed-call text-back?

Missed-call text-back is an automation that sends a text message to a caller within seconds of a call you did not answer. Instead of hitting silence, the caller receives a friendly message that acknowledges them and invites them to keep the conversation going by text.

It works because texting is easy. Someone who would not leave a voicemail will often reply to a text, especially one that arrives right after they called, while their question is still on their mind.

## Why it works so well for real estate

- **It keeps the lead engaged** in the moment, instead of letting them move on to the next agent.
- **It captures leads who never leave a voicemail**, which you might otherwise never know about.
- **It buys you time.** A quick text exchange can gather the basics so your callback is informed and productive.
- **It fits how people communicate.** Many buyers and sellers prefer texting for quick questions.

## What a good text-back message says

The first message sets the tone, so keep it short, warm, and clearly from you. A good message:

1. **Identifies you** by name and business, so they know who is texting.
2. **Acknowledges the call** so the text makes sense in context.
3. **Offers a clear next step**, like asking how you can help or when is a good time to talk.
4. **Includes opt-out language** where required.

Here is an example:

> Hi, this is Jordan with Coastal Home Group. Sorry I missed your call! I'm with a client right now. Are you calling about a home you'd like to see, or about selling? Reply STOP to opt out.

Avoid anything that feels like a generic marketing blast. The goal is to continue a conversation the caller started, not to pitch them.

## Take it further with conversation, not just a single text

A single automatic text is a strong start. The next level is having the text conversation continue intelligently. An AI-powered text-back can:

- Ask your qualifying questions, such as area, budget, timeline, and whether they are working with another agent.
- Answer common questions about a listing.
- Offer available times and book an appointment on your calendar.
- Log the entire conversation in your CRM and alert you when a lead is hot.

Paired with an [AI voice agent that answers calls 24/7](/blog/what-is-an-ai-voice-agent-for-real-estate), text-back becomes a safety net rather than the main line of defense: the agent answers most calls live, and text-back catches the rest.

## Compliance basics to keep in mind

Texting leads comes with rules, and it is worth getting them right. This is general information, not legal advice, so check with your broker or an attorney for your situation. In general:

- **Only text people who contacted you** or who have given consent to be texted.
- **Honor opt-outs immediately.** Anyone who replies STOP should not receive further texts.
- **Identify yourself clearly** in your messages.
- **Register your business texting** with the carriers through the required registration process (often called A2P 10DLC in the U.S.), which your texting provider can help with.
- **Keep messages relevant** to the caller's inquiry, and avoid unsolicited marketing.

A reputable provider will build these safeguards into the system for you.

## Setting it up

To set up missed-call text-back, you need a business phone number that can detect missed calls, a texting platform that is properly registered, and ideally a connection to your CRM so every conversation is saved. Many CRMs offer basic text-back, but the quality of the message, the follow-up logic, and the hand-off to you make the difference between a nice feature and a system that consistently saves leads.
